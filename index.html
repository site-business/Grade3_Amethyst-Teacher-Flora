💎 About Section Amethyst

Section Amethyst — named after the precious purple gemstone — represents our class's strength, wisdom, and clarity of thought. Just as amethyst is known for its beauty and resilience, we believe every student in this section possesses unique qualities that, when nurtured properly, will shine brilliantly. Our class is a community of young learners, thinkers, and dreamers who support one another on the journey of growth and discovery.
